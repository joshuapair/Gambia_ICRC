Good questions. Clear answers.

Before you take the next step.

Is this an official IC&RC member board?

This website does not claim IC&RC member-board status. The founders are developing an independent initiative. Any future affiliation or authority to award credentials will be stated only after it is confirmed.

Can I enroll or receive a credential now?

Enrollment, fees, training dates, and credential issuance are not yet announced. You can prepare an inquiry to discuss your interest. IC&RC credentials are administered through authorized member boards with their own requirements.

How is peer support different from counseling?

Our planned peer approach centers shared experience, choice, encouragement, and recovery goals. Counseling involves professional clinical skills and a separate scope of practice. A peer pathway does not automatically qualify someone to practice as a counselor.

Will a credential transfer internationally?

Reciprocity is not automatic. IC&RC facilitates transfers of eligible credentials between participating member-board jurisdictions, and the receiving board may impose additional requirements. No international transfer is promised by this initiative.

Who can help develop the initiative?

We welcome conversations with people with lived experience, educators, counselors, community organizations, supervisors, and potential local partners. Use the inquiry tool below to introduce yourself.

Where do the credential details come from?

The explorer links directly to official IC&RC pages, reviewed November 7, 2026. They summarize reference standards; always consult the current official standards and the relevant authorized board before making a credentialing decision.
